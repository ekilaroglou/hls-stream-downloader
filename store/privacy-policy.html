Privacy Policy — HLS Stream Downloader

Effective date: November 20, 2026

HLS Stream Downloader ("the extension") saves HLS video streams that the web page you are watching plays as a file on your computer. This policy explains what information the extension handles and what it does with it.

In short: everything happens in your browser. The extension does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else, and it has no servers, analytics, ads or tracking.

Session storage is held in memory by Chrome and is not readable by websites. The extension removes a tab's entries when the tab is closed, and Chrome clears all of it when the browser closes.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer, to third parties or to any server other than the website you are downloading a video from.
  • It does not collect personal information, browsing history, passwords, form data or payment information.
  • It does not read or change your cookies. When you download a video, the requests are made from the page that plays it, so the website receives the same cookies and headers it already receives from your browser while you watch.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code. All of its code is included in the extension package.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data.

Website requests made for downloads

Save loaded part takes only what the page already loaded from your browser's cache and makes no new requests to the website. When you click Download full video or Record, the extension requests the video's remaining segments from the website (or its content delivery network) that serves the video, in the same way the website's own video player does. Those requests are subject to that website's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Access to websites (all sites): videos can be on any website and are often served from other domains. This lets the extension detect playlists on the page you are watching and load the video from that page when you ask for it.
  • webRequest: to see when a page loads an HLS playlist, and which video parts it has finished loading.
  • scripting: to load the video from inside the page that plays it.
  • downloads: to save the finished file.
  • storage: to remember the videos and loaded parts found on open tabs and the details of a running download (session storage), and your settings.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received by the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide the extension's single purpose: saving video streams that you choose to download.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle information about children.
