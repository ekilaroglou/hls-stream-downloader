Settings

Download speed

Used by "Download full video" for the parts your browser hasn't loaded. Normal and Fast load about 30 seconds ahead like a video player, then keep a steady pace (at most 6 requests a second). Max loads as fast as it can. Every speed slows down by itself if a site says it's getting too many requests. You can also change it while a download runs.

Save to

Where finished videos are saved.

Saved

Everything happens in your browser. The extension sends nothing to its developer or anyone else.
